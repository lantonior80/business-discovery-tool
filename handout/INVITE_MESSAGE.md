## Tips
- **Send it to warm contacts first** (people who asked for help or know you). Cold owners rarely finish.
- **Follow up once** if the file hasn't arrived within a few days: "Want me to stay on the line while you do it? It's about 20 minutes."
- **Deliver the diagnosis quickly.** That promise is what makes owners finish, so keep it.
